DESCRIPTION
The Coffeebreak Jumbo WiFi Phone is the One Button phone made huge for tablets, TVs and anyone who wants big text and big buttons. It calls over WiFi with no SIM needed.
Features:
- Huge text and buttons, with a text size bar under the header that you can fold away
- High contrast mode
- On a tablet or TV turned sideways the menu moves to the side and contacts show in a grid
- One big call button, tap to call, tap again to hang up, hold for walkie talkie
- Contacts with photos and profiles, messages, social links and a QR code for your number
Real device: the Coffeebreak Jumbo Tablet, a Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ 11 inch WiFi tablet, sells for $1,772.95 AUD with the app and design set up.
Note: it is a WiFi only tablet with no SIM. It cannot call 000 or any emergency service.
