Simple steps for gentle self-massage. Go slowly and stop if anything hurts.

Neck and shoulders

  1. Sit tall and drop your shoulders.
  2. Place fingertips either side of your neck.
  3. Make slow small circles, 10 times.
  4. Squeeze each shoulder gently, then let go.
  5. Roll shoulders back 5 times.

Hands

  1. Press your thumb into the centre of the other palm.
  2. Make slow circles across the palm.
  3. Gently pull each finger from base to tip.
  4. Swap hands and repeat.
