Coffeebreak Time Zone Sim

Live a day as one of 10 Coffeebreak characters, each in their own world city and time zone.

- Pick Coffeebreak Girl (Newcastle NSW), Coffee Bean Buddy (Bogotá), Marshmallow Mug Pals (London), Coffeebreak Kitty (Tokyo), Outback Kangaroo (Perth), Blue Fluff (Reykjavík), Coffeebreak TV Star (Los Angeles), Bean (Nashville), Queen (Addis Ababa) or Espresso (Rome)
- The clock runs on that city's real local time, with day and night skies
- Look after energy, food, fun, friends and coffee
- Work your character's job, take coffee breaks, enjoy hobbies and sleep
- Call friends around the world. If it's the middle of the night where they live, you'll wake them up
- Fly to other cities and watch the clock jump to their local time
- Earn game coins, decorate your home and complete goals
- Saves on your device. Works on phone, tablet and computer, with a fullscreen button
