How to explore

Keyboard: W A S D or arrow keys to move, drag the view to look around, Shift to run, F to switch between walking and flying, Q and E to go up and down while flying. On a phone or tablet use the arrow pad and drag to look. With a VR headset press Enter VR, then use the left thumbstick to move and the right thumbstick to turn.

Mount Sugarloaf (Kapara in the Awabakal language) rises to 412 m in the Sugarloaf State Conservation Area, near West Wallsend, about 25 km west of Newcastle. The summit has television transmitters and views over Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland and Cessnock. Snow fell on the summit in July 1965 and again in 1975.

This is a stylised, approximate 3D model made from generated terrain around the real coordinates (summit 32.8817 S, 151.5344 E). It is for fun and planning only and is not survey or safety data. Track positions are approximate. Before visiting, check NSW National Parks alerts for closures, stay on formed tracks and take care near unfenced cliffs.
