How to use

Desktop: pick a tool from the library. Shapes, Props and Lights are placed where you click. 3D Brushes draw when you drag. Materials paint the object you click. Worlds change the sky, ground and fog.

Drag empty space or right drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, WASD to pan, Q and E for down and up. Move, Rotate, Scale and Delete edit objects. Delete key removes the selected object, R turns it, [ and ] resize it, Ctrl Z undoes.

VR: press Enter VR on a WebXR headset browser. Trigger places, paints or draws. Grip grabs and moves objects. Left stick walks. Right stick left and right changes tool, up and down changes category.

Scenes are saved only on this device.
