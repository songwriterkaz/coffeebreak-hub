FAQs

Who are the performers

All performers are adults (18 and over) with training in pole fitness and aerial performance. Shows are athletic and artistic, in fully clothed performance costumes, and suitable for all-ages events.

What does the venue need

A clear, level space about 2m by 2m, with a ceiling height of about 2.7m or more for a fixed pole. For outdoor events or low ceilings we can bring a portable stage pole, which needs a flat area about 3m across. Please mention access, stairs and power in your notes.

Is it safe

Poles are installed and checked by the performer before every show, with a safe clear zone around the performance area. We ask that guests stay out of the clear zone and do not use the pole.

Cancellations

The deposit is non-refundable. If you need to change your date, let us know as early as possible and we will try to move your booking. If a performer is unable to attend, we will offer a replacement or a new date.

Travel

Shows in the Newcastle area have no travel fee. Outside Newcastle, travel is charged per kilometre as listed above.
